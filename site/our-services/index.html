Our Services

Request an Appointment (219) 362-5205

Get treated like family at our unique dental practice.

There's more to dentistry than keeping teeth healthy and bright. At Porter Family Dentistry, you'll always be treated like family by our dedicated staff. We strive to treat the whole person – mind, body, and spirit – and it shows!

Someone will be in the room with you at all times, treating you with respect, care, and friendship. Afterward, we will accept all dental insurance plans and work hard as your dental insurance advocate. We will fight for you to get every penny from your insurance company!

Comprehensive Preventative Family Dentistry

  • Custom dentures and implant supported dentures (permanent dentures)
  • Tooth whitening and Nite White whitening gel
  • Crowns and bridges
  • Resin fillings
  • Periodontal cleanings
  • Nitrous oxide for painless dentistry

If you have any questions, concerns, or would like to schedule an appointment, please contact us today. We look forward to providing you with the personal care you deserve.
