## Welcome

At Porter Family Dentistry, we take every step possible to ensure you have the most comfortable visit possible, and that goes beyond our pain-free dentistry. We are backed by 35 years of service and our staff has a combined experience of 177 years, specializing in empathy and compassionate service.

Our goal is to assist each patient in achieving and maintaining long term dental health and a beautiful smile. Some of the services we provide include:

- **GentleWave [Root Canal Treatment](https://www.porterfamilydentistry.com/our-services/endodontics/root-canal-retreatment/)**
- **[Cleanings & Exams](https://www.porterfamilydentistry.com/our-services/cleanings-prevention/dental-exams-cleanings/)**
- [**Tooth Extractions**](https://www.porterfamilydentistry.com/our-services/cleanings-prevention/simple-tooth-extractions/)
- **[Dentures & Partial Dentures](https://www.porterfamilydentistry.com/our-services/cosmetic-dentistry/dentures-partial-dentures/)**
- **[Custom Teeth Whitening](https://www.porterfamilydentistry.com/our-services/cosmetic-dentistry/nitewhite-acp-teeth-whitening/)**
- **[Tooth Colored Fillings](https://www.porterfamilydentistry.com/our-services/cosmetic-dentistry/composite-fillings/)**
- **[Porcelain Crowns](https://www.porterfamilydentistry.com/our-services/restorations/crowns-caps/)**
- **[Dental Emergencies](https://www.porterfamilydentistry.com/our-services/dental-emergencies/) (For Patients of Record)**
- [**Additional Services**](https://www.porterfamilydentistry.com/OurServices.aspx)

[New patients click here](https://www.porterfamilydentistry.com/PatientInformation.aspx) to learn about appointments & payment options.

Our office is conveniently located for patients throughout the La Porte, Michigan City, Plymouth, and Knox areas, and we try to stay involved with our community outside of our regular dental services.

Please browse through our website and be sure to [contact us](https://www.porterfamilydentistry.com/ContactUs.aspx) if you have any questions or would like to schedule an appointment. You may also use our [online appointment request](https://www.porterfamilydentistry.com/AppointmentRequest.aspx) form to schedule your appointment.
